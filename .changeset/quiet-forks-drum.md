Add state-variant utility classes from PLTFRM-94106: `ui-hover:*`/`ui-disabled:*`/`ui-focus-visible:*`/`ui-last:*` forms of every semantic color class (516 new classes), generated by a generic class-list × variant-list multiplier rather than case-by-case.
